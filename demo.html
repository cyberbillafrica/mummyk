The Challenge

The WhatsApp Group is costing you sales.

Right now, selling on WhatsApp is chaotic for both you and your customers.

Security & Trust

Built to protect your business

  • Private Admin Portal: Only you can access the dashboard with your secret email and password.
  • Secure Payments: Customers cannot mark their own orders as "Paid". You have the final say.
  • Data Privacy: Customers cannot see other people's orders, phone numbers, or payment receipts.
  • Anti-Fraud: Because you verify the bank alert manually before confirming, no one can fake a payment screenshot to get free goods.

The Golden Rule

Never mark an order as "Paid" just because you see a screenshot. Always open your actual bank app first to confirm the money has landed. The portal is a tool; you are the boss.
