Help Guide

A quick manual for running your store from this dashboard.

1. Adding & Managing Products

  • Open the Manage Products tab to see every product currently live on the store, with its name, category, sizes, price and media.
  • Click Edit on any product to copy its details into a product form on the Add Products tab — update what you need and save. Saving overwrites the existing product with the same name (add a form whose name matches the one you are editing to update it; the rest of the forms are untouched).
  • Click Delete to remove a product from the store entirely — this deletes the product record and its media. This cannot be undone.
  • Click the × on a product's media thumbnail in the Manage Products table to remove just the photo or video without deleting the product itself.
  • To add brand new products, open the Add Products tab.
  • Fill in the product Name and Price in Naira (₦).
  • Choose a Category: Clothes, Shoes, Bags or Others.
  • Enter Sizes separated by commas (e.g. 2yrs, 4yrs, 6yrs). Leave blank if the product has no sizes.
  • Optionally paste a YouTube link to show a video on the product card.
  • Click Upload Media to add product photos or a short video. Files are saved to Cloudinary under mummyk/products.
  • Need more items? Click + Add More Product to get another form.
  • When you are done, click Save All Products. All products go live on the storefront at once.

2. Managing Orders

  • Open the Manage Orders tab to see every order.
  • Confirm Payment — click this after you have checked the payment proof and the money has arrived. It changes the payment status to Confirmed.
  • Reject Proof — click this if the uploaded proof is invalid or unclear. It changes the payment status to Rejected. You can then contact the customer via their phone number.
  • Mark Delivered — click this once the customer has received their order. It changes the order status to Delivered.
  • Click the link under Payment Proof to view the receipt the customer uploaded.

3. Reading Messages

  • Open the Messages tab to see customer complaints and enquiries.
  • Each message shows the customer's name, phone number and category (Complaint, Delivery, Payment, Product Enquiry, Other).
  • Click the status button to move a message through: New → In Progress → Resolved.
  • Use the phone number to call or WhatsApp the customer back.

4. Business Settings

  • WhatsApp Number — where customer WhatsApp orders are sent (international format, e.g. 2348012345678).
  • Bank Name / Account Name / Account Number — shown to customers who choose Bank Transfer at checkout.
  • Announcement Banner Text — shown in the gold banner on the storefront. Clear it and save to hide the banner.

💡 Good to Know

  • Orders start as New / Unpaid. Always verify the money before confirming payment.
  • This dashboard is protected by Firebase Auth — never share your login details.
  • Product photos and payment proofs are stored on Cloudinary; database records are stored on Firestore.
